Dose = MU × Sc(c) × Sp(sd) × TMR(d, sd) × (100 / (SSD + d))² × OAR, normalised to the calibration point. The detector reading includes volume averaging over the chamber's sensitive volume, so a Farmer chamber under-reads in small fields and in the build-up region. Values are realistic but are not VERT's own beam data.
